Are all listed projects built with Vibe Coding?

Yes. Everything we list is built with AI-assisted coding tools — whether a full product or a small tool, as long as it's made the Vibe Coding way, it can be submitted.
02

Why submit by email instead of a form?

To ensure quality, we currently use manual review. Submitting by email lets you include a more detailed project introduction and your build story.
03

Are links dofollow?

Yes, all listed project links are dofollow, giving great indie developers more exposure and SEO value.
04

Can I submit a project that's still in development?

Yes. We very much welcome "work in progress" projects, and will show them as "preview" with their development status marked.
